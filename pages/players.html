📖 How to read this page

  • Filters \u2013 at the top of the page. Quick range or the From / To dates limit every number to matches played in that period; Players limits it to the people you tick (the number beside each name is how many matches they played in the chosen period). Reset filters returns to everything. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
  • Data period \u2013 the first and last match date in the selection, with the number of team matches, days and player appearances. Every number covers our two teams' matches, all formats (6, 10, 15 and 20 overs) combined. Games a player took part in for other teams are not counted.
  • Summary cards – team totals across the shown players, plus the top run scorer, top wicket taker and the player with the highest form score.
  • Charts – the five highest run scorers and wicket takers.
  • Leader tiles – the top three players for one stat each. Some tiles need a minimum number of balls, innings or overs, so tiny samples don't win.
  • Squad Performance Matrix – one row per player. Click a column header to sort, click again to reverse. Type in the search box to filter by name.
  • Refresh reloads the latest data file. Updated shows the date the data file was generated. The data is scheduled to refresh every Sunday at 9 pm UK time.
  • Full dashboard opens the detailed analytics view (player profiles, squad builder, strategy).
  • Players with no matches in the period are not shown.

⭐ Form score (0–100)

Batting and bowling are each scored 0–100, then combined by role. Each uses four parts:

PartWeight
Recent form (last 5 matches)40%
Strike rate (batting) / economy (bowling)30%
Batting average / wickets taken20%
Consistency (steadiness of scores or economy)10%
  • Batting benchmarks: average 20 = full marks, strike rate 120 = full marks. Recent form blends 60% average and 40% strike rate.
  • Bowling benchmarks: economy 9 or better = full marks, 16 or worse = zero, linear in between. 15 wickets = full marks.
  • Bowling discipline: wides, no-balls and boundaries conceded per over move the bowling score by up to ±5.
  • Role: batsman = batting score; bowler = bowling score; all-rounder = 55% batting + 45% bowling; wicket-keeper = 75% batting + a keeping bonus.
  • Fielding bonus (up to +5) exists in the formula, but catches are not collected yet, so it adds nothing today.

🚦 Status badge

Compares the player's last 5 matches with their own career:

score = 0.6 × (recent avg ÷ career avg) + 0.4 × (recent SR ÷ career SR)

  • In Form  score 1.2 or more
  • Average  between 0.6 and 1.2
  • Out of Form  score 0.6 or less
  • Insufficient Data  no career batting average yet

Status is about batting form only, while the Form score also includes bowling, so a bowler can have a high score and a modest status.

⚠️ Good to know

  • Small samples swing the numbers: a player with 2 innings can top a list. Check M and Inn before drawing conclusions.
  • Fielding stats (catches, dropped catches) and MVP points are not available in the data, so they are not shown.
  • Numbers come from CricHeroes scorecards. If a scorecard is missing or incomplete there, it is missing here.
